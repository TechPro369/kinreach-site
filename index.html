KinReach is a quiet safety net. Set a check-in timer, and if you ever miss it, we automatically email the one person you trust — so you're never silently unreachable.

How it works

Silence is the signal

As long as you check in, nothing happens and no one is bothered. It's only your absence that triggers an alert.

3

A tap resets the timer. Do it whenever you're okay.

4

If you go silent

Miss a deadline and we email your contact automatically — even if your phone is off.

Who it's for

Peace of mind, for the people who need it

People who live alone and want someone alerted if they go quiet.

Anyone keeping a caring eye on an elderly parent or relative.

Solo travelers and hikers heading into higher-risk situations.

Anyone who just wants one trusted person notified if they can't respond.

Privacy first

We collect as little as possible

KinReach is designed to know almost nothing about you. The only thing it needs is a schedule and one email address.

  • No location tracking. Ever.
  • No ads and no third-party trackers.
  • Your contact's details are encrypted.
  • Delete your data anytime, right in the app.
