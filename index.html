The short version

KinReach is a personal check-in app. You set a schedule, and if you don't check in on time, we send an email to the one emergency contact you chose. To do that, we store only what's needed: your check-in schedule and status, and your emergency contact's email address (encrypted). We do not collect your location, we do not track you, we do not run ads, and we do not sell your data. You can delete your data at any time from within the app.

This Privacy Policy explains how KinReach (“KinReach,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information when you use the KinReach mobile application and related services (the “Service”). KinReach is operated by GeezLabs (the “Operator”). By using the Service, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information we collect

KinReach is built around data minimization: we only collect what the check-in and alerting feature actually requires. Specifically:

WhatWhy we need it
Emergency contact email (and optional contact name)So we can email that person if you miss a check-in. This is stored encrypted.
Your check-in activity — your chosen interval, the time of your last check-in, your deadline, and whether a session is active, stopped, or expiredTo run the countdown and decide when an alert should be sent.
A device session token (stored as a one-way hash on our servers)To securely link your app to your check-in session without requiring an account, password, or personal login.
Your display name (optional)If you provide it, it is included in the alert so your contact knows who the alert is about.
Basic delivery/diagnostic logsMinimal technical logs to operate the Service, detect abuse, and confirm whether an alert email was sent.

What we do not collect: We do not collect your GPS or precise location, your device's contact list, your photos, your microphone or camera, advertising identifiers, or third-party analytics/tracking data. KinReach contains no ads no trackers no location.

2. How we use your information

We use the information above only to:

  • Run your check-in timer and determine when a deadline has passed;
  • Send the alert email to your chosen emergency contact when you miss a check-in;
  • Send a test alert when you request one;
  • Keep the Service secure, reliable, and free from abuse.

We do not use your information for advertising, profiling, or sale to third parties.

3. How your information is stored and protected

  • Encryption in transit: All communication between the app and our servers uses HTTPS (TLS).
  • Encryption at rest: Your emergency contact details are encrypted on our servers using AES-256-GCM. API responses only ever return a masked version of the address.
  • On-device security: Your session token and contact information are stored on your device using the platform's secure storage (Apple Keychain on iOS, Android Keystore on Android).

An honest note on encryption: This is encryption at rest, not “zero-knowledge” encryption. Because the Service has to actually send an email to your contact, our server must be able to decrypt the contact's email address at the moment an alert is sent. We tell you this plainly so you can make an informed choice.

4. Service providers we rely on

We use a small number of trusted third-party providers to operate the Service. They process data only to provide their service to us and are not permitted to use it for their own purposes:

  • Railway — cloud hosting and database infrastructure (United States), where the Service and its encrypted data are stored.
  • Resend — transactional email delivery, used to send the alert and test emails to your emergency contact.

5. Your emergency contact

KinReach lets you enter one emergency contact's email address so we can notify them if you miss a check-in. You are responsible for making sure you have that person's permission to store their email address and to send them alerts. Your contact will only ever receive: a test email (when you send one), or an alert email when you miss a check-in. These emails clearly identify KinReach and, where a test, state plainly that it is only a test.

We keep your data only while it is needed to provide the Service:

  • You can delete your KinReach data at any time from within the app. If you would like us to delete any remaining data associated with you, contact us at the address below and we will do so.
