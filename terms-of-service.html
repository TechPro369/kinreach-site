1. What KinReach does

KinReach is a “check-in” app, sometimes called a dead-man's switch. You choose a check-in interval (for example, every 24 hours). If you check in before your deadline, nothing happens. If your deadline passes without a check-in, our server sends an alert email to the single emergency contact you have chosen, letting them know you missed your check-in so they can reach out to you. You can start, stop, and change your session at any time.
